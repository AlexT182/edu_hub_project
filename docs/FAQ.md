# FAQ

## What is EduHub?

EduHub is a hosted web platform focused on study-abroad discussion, knowledge sharing, AI-assisted Q&A, and admin-side lead handling.

## Who is it for?

Primary audiences include:

- students exploring study-abroad options,
- parents researching risks, costs, scholarships, and visa paths,
- operators or consulting teams managing moderation and follow-up.

## Is EduHub only a forum?

No. EduHub combines:

- a public community layer,
- an AI chatbot/help layer,
- an admin/operations layer.

## Does EduHub include an admin panel?

Yes. The live product includes admin-side surfaces for dashboard, posts, users, topics, leads, chat history, and settings.

## Is the AI chatbot public-facing?

Yes, the product includes an AI-assisted Q&A layer for study-abroad-related questions and consultation handoff flows.

## Does this repository contain the source code?

No. This is a public product-information and support repository only.

## Can users browse without full admin access?

Yes. EduHub has a public-facing community surface, while admin pages are access-controlled separately.

## Is EduHub self-hosted by end users?

No public self-hosted distribution flow is documented here.
