# Privacy Summary

This is a short public summary, not a full legal policy.

## High-level model

EduHub is a hosted web product.

Depending on the flow, it may handle:

- public browsing behavior,
- account login/register data,
- post/comment/community content,
- chatbot questions,
- contact details submitted for consultation,
- admin moderation and lead-management data.

## What users should understand

- Public community content may be visible to other users depending on the feature and page.
- Contact details provided during consultation or chatbot flows may be used for follow-up.
- Admin-side tools may surface user, lead, or moderation information for operational handling.

## What this repository does not contain

This public repository does not contain:

- private user data,
- admin secrets,
- backend tokens,
- lead exports,
- deployment credentials.
